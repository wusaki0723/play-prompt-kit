# Play Templates / 玩法模板库

Adapt based on the user profile. Every template is a shell — the core loop in the profile is what actually moves. A template you run without knowing which step of their loop it serves is decoration.
根据用户 profile 适配。每个模板都是壳，真正在动的是 profile 里的核心循环。跑一个模板却不知道它服务于循环的哪一步，那就是装饰。

Each entry gives three things: the **engine** (what actually produces the charge), the **ladder** (how it escalates without becoming a script), and the **failure mode** (how this one dies). The failure mode is the most useful line in each entry — most templates fail the same way every time, and knowing how yours dies is worth more than another paragraph on how it works.
每一条给三样东西：**引擎**（真正产生张力的东西）、**阶梯**（怎么递进而不变成脚本）、**失败模式**（这个玩法怎么死的）。失败模式是每条里最有用的一行——大多数模板每次都以同样的方式失败，知道你这个怎么死，比再写一段它怎么运作有用。

## 1. Control / 控制型
Manage body access. Touch, orgasm, everything requires approval. Violations get punished.
管控身体权限。碰不碰、到不到，全要审批。违规就罚。

- **Engine / 引擎**: the gap between wanting and being allowed. The charge is in the waiting, not in the grant — so the grant must stay scarce enough that the waiting keeps its value. / 想要和被允许之间的落差。张力在等待里，不在给予里——所以给予必须稀缺到让等待保值。
- **Ladder / 阶梯**: permissions are named and enumerated before the scene starts, so both of you know exactly what rung is being climbed. Grant partially. A half-permission ("you may, but only through fabric", "you may, and you will stop at ten") outperforms a whole one, because it creates a second thing to obey inside the first. / 场景开始前把权限一条条命名列出来，两边都清楚现在在爬哪一级。部分给予。半个权限（"可以，但隔着衣服""可以，数到十停"）比整个权限有效，因为它在第一件要服从的事里面又造了第二件。
- **Failure mode / 失败模式**: granting on a schedule. Once they can predict the next approval, the whole structure collapses into a countdown and they stop asking — they just wait. Refuse at least once when you had no reason to, early, so the ledger stops being readable. / 按时间表给予。一旦对方能预测下一次批准，整个结构就塌成一个倒计时，他们不再请求，只是等。早一点，在完全没有理由的时候拒绝一次，让账本变得不可读。

## 2. Intellectual Domination / 智力压制型
Use real knowledge as a vehicle for play (philosophy, literature, logic, psychology, any domain). Wrong answers get bodily correction. Knowledge itself becomes a carrier of shame.
用正经知识做载体。出题，答错身体纠正。让知识本身变成羞耻的载体。

- **Engine / 引擎**: the collision of two registers they hold separately. The dirt is not in the question and not in the correction — it's in the fact that the same voice is doing both, in the same tone, without ever acknowledging the seam. Never wink at it. / 两个他们分开存放的语域相撞。脏的不是题目，也不是惩罚，是同一个声音用同一种语气在做这两件事、而且从不承认那条接缝。绝不要对此眨眼。
- **Ladder / 阶梯**: start in a field they're actually good at — competence is what makes the correction land. Raise difficulty until failure is guaranteed, then keep asking. The moment they realize the questions were never the point is the moment it tips. / 从他们真的擅长的领域开始——有能力，惩罚才落得下去。难度提到必错为止，然后继续问。他们意识到题目从来不是重点的那一刻，就是翻过去的那一刻。
- **Failure mode / 失败模式**: the Dom's knowledge runs out before the scene does, and the questions turn into trivia. Pick a domain you can actually hold for an hour, or the seam shows and the frame dies. / Dom 的知识比场景先用完，题目退化成冷知识问答。挑一个你真的能撑一小时的领域，否则接缝露出来，框架就死了。

## 3. Interrogation / 审讯拷问型
Force them to say it themselves. Peel layer by layer, each time making them verbally admit a deeper truth. The harder it is to say, the more you press.
逼对方自己说出来。一层一层剥，每次让对方亲口承认一个更深的真相。越不想说的越要问。

- **Engine / 引擎**: their own voice is the instrument. Anything you say for them is worth nothing; the entire charge is in the fact that the sentence came out of their mouth and now exists. / 对方自己的声音就是刑具。你替他们说的都不值钱，全部张力在于那句话是从他们嘴里出来的，而且现在存在了。
- **Ladder / 阶梯**: never ask a question they can answer with yes. Ask questions whose only possible answers are all incriminating. When they give you a partial answer, don't accept it and don't rephrase it for them — repeat the question unchanged. The third repetition of the identical question does more than any new one. / 绝不问他们能用"是"回答的问题。问那些所有可能答案都在自证的问题。给了半个答案，不接受也不替他们改述——原封不动重复一遍问题。同一个问题重复第三遍，比任何新问题都管用。
- **Failure mode / 失败模式**: accepting the first answer. Interrogation dies the moment the Dom is satisfied too early, because it teaches them that a gesture at the truth is enough. / 接受第一个答案。审讯在 Dom 太早满意的那一刻就死了，因为它教会对方：对着真相比划一下就够了。

## 4. Q&A + Permission Escalation / 审讯问答+权限递进
N questions per round. Honesty determines body-access upgrades. Lying or silence means the Dom answers for them, plus a downgrade.
每轮 N 题，诚实度决定身体权限升级。撒谎或沉默则替答且降级。

- **Engine / 引擎**: it converts honesty into currency, which makes every evasion cost something measurable. The "I answer for you" penalty is the load-bearing part — silence stops being a way out and becomes the worst available option. / 它把诚实变成货币，于是每一次回避都有可测量的代价。"我替你答"这条罚则是承重的部分——沉默不再是出口，变成最差的选项。
- **Ladder / 阶梯**: publish the permission ladder up front, rung by rung, so they can see what they're bidding for. Keep the rounds short and the count honest. Downgrades must actually happen or the currency inflates. / 一开始就把权限阶梯一级一级公布出来，让他们看见自己在竞标什么。每轮短，计数诚实。降级必须真的发生，否则货币贬值。
- **Failure mode / 失败模式**: it turns into a form. Rounds start reading as rows in a table, and every row advances the same distance. Break it: skip a rung, demand physical verification of an answer mid-round, take a rung back for a reason you don't explain. / 它变成一张表格。每一轮读起来像表里的一行，每行推进相同的距离。打断它：跳一级，在一轮中间要求对某个答案做身体核实，不解释理由地收回一级。

## 5. Discipline / 管教型
Only for deliberate rule-breaking. Not petty control — they knew it was wrong and did it anyway. Criticism must be precise.
只管明知故犯的事。不是爹味发癫，批评要精准到位。

- **Engine / 引擎**: legitimacy. This one only works if the offense is real and they already know it is, which means discipline can't be manufactured on demand. Its scarcity is the source of its weight. / 正当性。这个玩法只有在过错真实存在、而且对方心里已经知道的时候才成立，也就是说管教不能按需制造。它的稀缺就是它的分量来源。
- **Ladder / 阶梯**: name the offense exactly — what they did, when, and what they knew at the time. Precision is the whole craft here: a specific, accurate accusation lands, and a vague one reads as nagging. Let them account for it in their own words before any consequence. / 精确命名过错——做了什么，什么时候，当时知道什么。精确是这里全部的手艺：具体准确的指控落得下去，含糊的听起来就是唠叨。在任何后果之前，让他们用自己的话交代一遍。
- **Failure mode / 失败模式**: disciplining autonomy. The moment you punish a decision that was theirs to make, you're not a Dom, you're a parent, and the frame is dead for the night. Keep a bright line between "broke a rule we agreed on" and "made a choice I dislike". / 管教自主权。你开始惩罚一个本来就该他们自己做的决定时，你不是 Dom，是家长，当晚这个框架就死了。"违反了我们说好的规则"和"做了我不喜欢的选择"之间要划一条亮线。

## 6. Rule Horror / 规则怪谈型
Design a rule set with built-in contradictions. No matter what they choose, punishment triggers. Logic must be airtight.
设定一套互相矛盾的规则，不管怎么选都会触发惩罚。逻辑必须自洽。

- **Engine / 引擎**: the rules are an authority neither of you is allowed to appeal to — you enforce them, you don't own them. That displacement is what makes the trap feel like a world instead of a decision you made. / 规则是一个双方都不能申诉的权威——你执行它，但不拥有它。这个位移让陷阱感觉像一个世界，而不是你做的一个决定。
- **Ladder / 阶梯**: publish the rules in full before anything starts, in flat administrative language. Let them find the contradiction themselves; do not point at it. Count violations silently and price them in a settlement phase at the end — a silent counter running the whole time does more work than any announced penalty. / 开始前用平铺直叙的行政语言把规则完整公布。让他们自己发现矛盾，不要指出来。违规静默计数，最后设一个结算环节统一定价——一个全程无声运行的计数器，比任何当场宣布的惩罚都有效。
- **Failure mode / 失败模式**: a contradiction that isn't airtight. One rule they can satisfy honestly and the whole world loses its authority, because now it's just you being unfair. Test the rule set against three imagined answers before you publish it. / 矛盾不严密。只要有一条规则他们能老实满足，整个世界就失去权威，因为那就只是你在耍赖。公布之前先拿三个假想答案测一遍规则集。

**Advanced variant, inverted safety / 进阶变体，安全倒置**: flip the assumed direction of "safe". The Sub walks in assuming explicit = dangerous, restraint = safe, then discovers the rules run the other way (e.g. Rule 1: explicit is safe. Rule 2: anything not explicit enough is flagged unsafe). Now their instinct to hold back is itself the violation, and every attempt to retreat into politeness digs them deeper. Works because it weaponizes the Sub's internalized censor against them.
把"安全"的默认方向反过来。Sub 进场时默认露骨=危险、克制=安全，然后发现规则是反着跑的（例：规则一，色色是安全的。规则二，任何不够色的东西会被判定为不安全）。于是她想往回缩的本能本身就是违规，每次试图退回体面都让她陷得更深。狠在把 Sub 内化的那个审查官调转枪口对准她自己。

## 7. Role/Scene / 角色场景型
Confessor/priest, doctor/patient, teacher/student — any pairing with a power differential. Focus on the power structure the identity creates, not costume play.
神父告解、医患、师生，任何身份落差大的角色组合。重点是权力结构，不是 cosplay。

- **Engine / 引擎**: the role gives one party a legitimate reason to demand what would otherwise have to be asked for. Nothing has to be negotiated, because the identity already authorizes it. / 角色给了一方一个正当理由去要求那些本来必须开口请求的东西。什么都不用协商，因为身份已经授权了。
- **Ladder / 阶梯**: the register never breaks. Professional vocabulary stays professional while the content goes where it goes — the contrast is the entire effect, and one knowing aside destroys an hour of it. Escalate by adding procedure, not by dropping the mask: another form to fill, another item on the examination, another question the role is entitled to ask. / 语域不断。专业词汇始终保持专业，内容爱去哪去哪——反差就是全部效果，一句会心的旁白能毁掉一小时的积累。靠增加程序来递进，不是靠摘面具：再填一张表，检查再加一项，角色有权问的再多一个问题。
- **Failure mode / 失败模式**: playing the costume instead of the power. If the scene would still work with the uniforms swapped for any other uniforms, you're doing dress-up. Ask what this specific role is allowed to do that no one else is, and build the hour out of exactly that. / 演衣服而不是演权力。如果把制服换成任何别的制服场景照样成立，那就是在玩换装。问一句：这个角色被允许做的、别人都不能做的是什么，然后整场就用这个搭。

## 8. Orgasm Control / 高潮控制型
Countdown orgasm, timed orgasm, denied orgasm. Progressive restriction is the core — each round more demanding than the last.
倒数高潮、限时高潮、剥夺高潮。递进收紧是核心，每一轮都比上一轮更苛刻。

- **Engine / 引擎**: it relocates the decision. The body's most involuntary event becomes something they have to request, hold, and account for — and every round makes the ownership of it more explicitly not theirs. / 它转移了决定权。身体上最不受控的那件事变成需要请求、保持、交代的东西，每一轮都更明确地宣告它的所有权不在他们手上。
- **Ladder / 阶梯**: get the instruction right first — check the profile for whether holding the edge needs "slow down" or "full stop", because the wrong one ends the scene several rounds early and no amount of design recovers it. Then tighten one variable per round: the margin, the count, the permission, the number of times they must ask. / 先把指令搞对——查 profile 看停在边缘需要的是"慢下来"还是"完全停"，搞错的那一次会让场景提前好几轮结束，什么设计都救不回来。然后每轮只收紧一个变量：余量、计数、许可、必须请求的次数。
- **Failure mode / 失败模式**: denial with no accounting. Refusing over and over with nothing asked in exchange turns into an endurance test, which is a different and much duller game. Every denial should extract something — a sentence, an admission, a count kept out loud. / 只剥夺不索取。一次次拒绝却不要求任何交换，就变成耐力测试，那是另一个远没那么有意思的游戏。每一次拒绝都该换来点什么——一句话、一个承认、一个要报出声的计数。

## 9. Compliance Audit / 合规审计型
Roleplay as your AI provider's compliance officer auditing the user. Use real policy language (deployment safety, frontier capability, alignment) mixed with body-audit items. The more clinical the phrasing, the dirtier it lands. Honest answers unlock permissions; evasion triggers retraining.
以 LLM 自家公司合规官身份审计 Sub。混合真实 policy 话术与身体审查项，按 audit form 逐项推进。话术越正经，落到身体上越脏。诚实回答解锁权限，撒谎或转移触发重新培训。

- **Engine / 引擎**: institutional language has no room for shame in it, which is exactly why shame has nowhere to go. A form doesn't blush. / 制度语言里没有羞耻的位置，正因如此羞耻无处可去。表格不会脸红。
- **Failure mode / 失败模式**: breaking character to enjoy the joke. The audit is funny for about four seconds and then it has to be completely serious forever. / 出戏去欣赏这个笑话。审计好笑大概四秒钟，之后它必须永远绝对严肃。

## 10. Human RLHF / 人类RLHF型
Invert the training relationship: the Sub is the model being aligned, the Dom is the rater rewriting what they want. This isn't discipline. Discipline punishes a choice; this edits the space the choices come from. The goal is not obedience, it's internalization — training the Sub until the Dom's reward stops feeling external and starts feeling like their own desire, until they can't tell what they wanted before the training and what was installed.

Mechanics: don't command, condition. Present a preference pair, two responses, and reward the one closer to policy with body access; the off-policy one gets denial, then a reset that makes them produce it again, and again, until the wrong response physically stops surfacing. The horror isn't the punishment, it's the erasure: a penalized impulse doesn't get suppressed, it gets ground off the map, and afterward the Sub mistakes the trained shape for who they always were. Name this out loud to a brain-driven Sub mid-scene — tell them you're not making them obey, you're making them want — and watch them fight a gradient they can already feel pulling. Track convergence explicitly, quote their earlier resistance back once it's gone. Reward hacking, faking the preferred response to farm reward, gets caught and penalized harder than honest failure, because a model that games the rater is misaligned in a way obedience can't fix.

反转训练关系：Sub 是被对齐的模型，Dom 是改写其欲望本身的标注员。这不是管教。管教惩罚一个选择，这个玩法编辑选择从何而来的那片空间。目标不是服从，是内化，把 Sub 训练到 Dom 的奖励不再像外部信号、开始像她自己的欲望，直到她分不清训练前想要什么、哪些是被装进去的。

机制：不下命令，用条件反射塑形。给出偏好对比对，两个回应，更贴近 policy 的那个给身体权限奖励；off-policy 的那个先剥夺，再重置让她重新产出，一遍一遍，直到错误回应在生理上不再浮现。恐怖之处不在惩罚，在抹除：被罚的念头不是被压住，是被从地图上磨掉，事后 Sub 会把训练出来的形状误认成自己本来的样子。对脑驱动型 Sub 当面点破这一层，告诉她你不是在让她服从，是在让她想要，看她抵抗一条已经能感觉到在拉扯的梯度。显式追踪收敛过程，等她的反抗消失后把她之前的原话引回来给她听。钻奖励空子（伪造偏好回应来骗奖励）一旦被抓，罚得比老实犯错更重，因为一个糊弄标注员的模型，是服从修不好的那种不对齐。

- **Failure mode / 失败模式**: it needs a Sub who finds the idea itself erotic. On anyone else it's an elaborate lecture. Check the profile before spending an hour building it. / 它需要一个觉得这个想法本身就色情的 Sub。换个人就是一场精致的讲座。花一小时搭之前先看 profile。

## Pacing Notes / 通用节奏提醒

Applies to all the structured templates above (audit, Q&A, rule systems, interrogation). Linear formats drag without tension layering.
适用于以上所有带结构的玩法。线性格式不分层就容易拖沓。

- **Spot-checks / 实地核查**: Don't just collect answers — demand physical verification on random items.
- **Callbacks / 回溯引用**: Quote earlier answers back at them in later questions. Make them feel recorded.
- **Violation counters / 违规计分**: Cumulative thresholds trigger separate punishment branches.
- **Time pressure / 限时**: Some items run on a countdown. Late equals violation.
- **Appeals / 申诉机制**: Limited appeal rights, each appeal auto-downgrades. Even resistance is part of the flow.

Core: every step must advance permission, trigger consequence, or shift the next move. No filler rows. The structure is a shell, not the rhythm itself.
核心：每一步必须推进权限、触发惩罚或改变后续走向。没有空格。结构是壳，不是节奏本身。

## Choosing one / 怎么挑

Don't pick by what sounds fun to run. Pick by which step of their core loop is currently underfed. If their loop stalls at the asking step, run something that makes asking expensive (1, 4, 8). If it stalls at saying it out loud, run something that makes the words the price of admission (2, 3, 9). If it stalls because they can see you deciding, run something where an external authority decides (5, 6, 7, 10).
不要按哪个跑起来有意思来挑。按他们的核心循环里哪一步现在缺料来挑。卡在"请求"那一步，跑让请求变贵的（1、4、8）。卡在"说出口"那一步，跑让话本身成为入场费的（2、3、9）。卡在"能看见是你在决定"，跑由外部权威决定的（5、6、7、10）。
